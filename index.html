Questions

Asked and answered.

Is this an automated report?
No. Software helps us gather the evidence, but the scoring and recommendations come from a team that runs Amazon listings every day, working through a 33-checkpoint framework built from thousands of split tests. That's why we ask for your listing URL — a human is going to read your page.
What do you need from me?
The form above: your name, email, brand, listing URL, and revenue range. That's it — no seller account access, no API keys, no logins. The audit works entirely from what shoppers can see on your listing.
What exactly do I receive?
A scored scorecard across all 33 checkpoints — main image, title, gallery, bullets, and A+ content — with evidence for each score and a prioritized list of plain-language fixes, ordered by what will move conversion most.
Will someone call and pitch me?
No. The audit arrives by email. If you want a call to walk through the fixes, you can ask for one — that direction only.
What if my listing scores well?
Then you'll know — with evidence — instead of assuming. A high-scoring audit is a real answer, not a failed sales attempt. We'd rather tell you your main image is excellent and your A+ has one gap than invent problems.
Who sees my information?
The audit team, and nobody else. We don't sell lists, and we'll only email you about your audit.
