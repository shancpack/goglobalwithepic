FAQ

Common questions

How is 3PL pricing calculated at Epic?
From five parts: receiving (per pallet or box), storage (per pallet position or bin, monthly), fulfillment (per order plus per extra unit, or per case or pallet), order entry ($0 by integration, $2 if we enter it), and labor for special projects ($35/hour). Our pricing guide walks through each one.
Is shipping included in the order price?
No. Postage and packaging are billed on top of the pick and pack rate, unless you provide your own packaging or ship on your own carrier accounts through ShipHero.
Which sales channels can you connect to?
Anything ShipHero integrates with, including Shopify and Amazon. You can also enter orders yourself in your portal or send them through the ShipHero API at no extra charge.
Can you prep and ship to Amazon FBA?
Yes. We ship case and pallet orders to Amazon and can apply FNSKU labels, bundle or bag units as labor. Pre-labeled units from your factory skip that cost. See FBA, FBM, or both?
How do I send inventory in?
Create an ASN in ShipHero listing the SKUs and quantities, label every carton and pallet, and keep pallets at or under 60 inches tall. Taller pallets are rebuilt as two and stored as two. Full checklist in our inbound guide.
How often am I billed?
Storage is calculated monthly and billed every two weeks, along with receiving, fulfillment and any project work from that period.
