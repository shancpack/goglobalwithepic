7 signs you've outgrown fulfilling orders yourself

Garage shelves, packing nights and climbing error rates. The common signals that it's time to hand fulfillment to a 3PL, and how to tell if you're actually ready.

Epic Warehouse Operations · November 4, 2026 · 5 min read

Most brands start by packing orders themselves: a spare room, then a garage, then a small unit with a rented forklift. That's the right call early on. But there's a point where the time and mistakes cost more than outsourcing would. Fulfillment operators who've watched hundreds of brands make the jump describe a consistent set of warning signs.1,2,3

1. You're running out of room

Inventory has spread from the closet to the garage to a storage unit, and finding a SKU means moving three others. Space you're improvising is space you can't scale.

2. Packing has become a full-time job

One owner described in Fulfillrite's roundup went from 10 to 150 orders a week and ended up spending four to five hours a day packing and shipping.1 Those are hours not spent on product, marketing or wholesale accounts.

3. Hiring and managing warehouse help is a headache

Recruiting, training and scheduling packers adds overhead, and labor costs spike exactly when sales do. A 3PL turns that into a variable, per-order cost.

4. Shipping errors are creeping up

Wrong item, wrong address, missing insert. Experts cited by Fulfillrite flag an error rate above 1 to 2 percent as a warning sign.1 Each mistake costs a reshipment, a return and sometimes a review.

5. Peak season breaks your process

Q4, a viral post or a big promotion turns a manageable week into a backlog. Rushing makes errors worse, and onboarding a 3PL in the middle of a spike is the hardest time to do it. The best time to move is a few months before your peak.

6. Your inventory counts don't match reality

Spreadsheet tracking drifts. You oversell items you don't have and miss sales on items you do. Inventory accuracy below 98 percent is another commonly cited red flag.1 A warehouse management system with scan-based picking fixes this at the source.

7. Customers are noticing

Late shipments, damaged boxes and wrong items show up in reviews and repeat purchase rates long before they show up in your P&L.

Are you actually ready?

Outsourcing doesn't fix everything. You'll get the most out of a 3PL if:

  • Your orders come through a platform that integrates (Shopify, Amazon and others through ShipHero), not email.
  • Your SKUs have barcodes, or you're ready to add them.
  • You know your rough monthly volume and units per order.
  • You've priced the switch honestly. Our fee-by-fee guide and estimator can help.
